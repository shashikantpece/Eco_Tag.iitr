One scan tells you the story

EcoTag gives every tagged tree and every community dog on campus its own page. Students, staff and visitors can learn about their green and furry neighbours, and the people who look after them can keep records in one shared place instead of scattered notes.

QR tags

Each tree and dog carries a QR code. Scanning it opens a page with its photo, species, status and location.

Species knowledge

Trees link to a catalog of {{stats.plants}} plants from the campus biodiversity report, with their uses and where they grow.

Care and safety

Vaccination and fertiliser history with reminders, and a quick way for anyone to report an injured or sick animal.

Campus map

See where every tagged tree and dog lives, search by name or species, and switch to satellite view.

How it works

From a walk around campus to a page anyone can open.

1

Tag

Volunteers walk the campus, photograph each tree or dog, note the species and drop a GPS pin.

2

Scan

A QR code on the tag opens the entry on any phone, with no app to install.

3

Care

Caretakers log vaccinations and treatments, and the community reports problems early.
